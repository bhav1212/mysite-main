<p class="post-typo-sub">The Train-First Itinerary</p>

<p>Switzerland is the country that makes you suddenly aware of how much grime your home city has accumulated. Trains arrive on time. Lake water is the colour of a designer's swatch. Mountains rise so abruptly that you can take a train from a city centre and be on a glacier in two hours. The route below leans into the trains.</p>
<h2 id="section-1">Route, day by day</h2>
<p><strong>Day 1 — Zürich.</strong> Land. Walk Niederdorf, the lakefront, and the Bahnhofstrasse. Dinner at Zeughauskeller. An early night; tomorrow is a travel day.</p>
<p><strong>Day 2 — Glacier Express to Zermatt.</strong> The 8-hour panoramic train from Zürich/Chur to Zermatt. Window seat. Two coffees. Watch the Alps unfold.</p>
<p><strong>Day 3 — Zermatt.</strong> Gornergrat railway at dawn for the Matterhorn view. Hike a short trail down. Afternoon at a lakeside, evening dinner with a view.</p>
<p><strong>Day 4 — Lucerne.</strong> Train back via Lucerne. The Chapel Bridge, lake walk, Mt. Pilatus cable car if time. Train back to Zürich for the flight out.</p>
<h2 id="section-2">Practical notes</h2>
<ul>
<li><strong>The Swiss Travel Pass</strong> pays for itself fast if you're doing 3+ scenic-train legs.</li>
<li><strong>Glacier Express tickets:</strong> book months ahead in summer; the panoramic carriages sell out.</li>
<li><strong>Cost:</strong> Switzerland is honest about being expensive. €15 for a coffee + croissant is normal in Zürich.</li>
<li><strong>Cash vs card:</strong> contactless is universal. Carry CHF only for vending machines.</li>
<li><strong>Hike:</strong> even a 30-minute trail near Gornergrat or Pilatus is more memorable than another city walk.</li>
</ul>
<h2 id="section-3">What I'd write more about</h2>
<ul>
<li>Glacier Express vs Bernina Express: which to pick.</li>
<li>Why Switzerland's transit feels like a public-good design treatise.</li>
<li>The Zermatt-vs-Grindelwald decision for first-timers.</li>
</ul>

    day with photos, specific places, and longer reflections as I revisit my notes.
