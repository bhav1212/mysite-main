<p class="post-typo-sub">Paris in Three, Provence in Three</p>

<p>France is the country where any honest post has to start with 'I know, I know.' Paris in three days is a cliché. I did it anyway. The unexpected bit was Provence — quiet, lavender-coloured, slower than Paris by a factor of ten. The route below is what I'd recommend to make the trip feel less greatest-hits-y.</p>
<h2 id="section-1">Route, day by day</h2>
<p><strong>Day 1 — Paris, Marais.</strong> Land, settle in the Marais. Walk the neighbourhood: Place des Vosges, the Jewish quarter, falafel at l'As du Fallafel. Sunset on the Île Saint-Louis.</p>
<p><strong>Day 2 — Paris museums.</strong> Louvre at opening, two hours, leave. Lunch in the Tuileries. Musée d'Orsay in the afternoon. Late evening at Sacré-Cœur.</p>
<p><strong>Day 3 — Paris, slower.</strong> Latin Quarter walk, Luxembourg Gardens, Shakespeare and Company, a long lunch. Eiffel Tower from Trocadéro at sunset (you don't need to go up).</p>
<p><strong>Day 4 — Paris → Avignon.</strong> TGV south (3 hours). Avignon is small, walled, beautiful. The Palais des Papes is bigger than its photos suggest.</p>
<p><strong>Days 5–6 — Provence.</strong> Day trip to the Pont du Gard, Les Baux-de-Provence, and a lavender field if it's June–August. End in Aix-en-Provence for a final dinner.</p>
<h2 id="section-2">Practical notes</h2>
<ul>
<li><strong>Métro</strong> is your friend in Paris; carnet of 10 tickets is more economical than singles.</li>
<li><strong>TGV bookings:</strong> book Paris → Avignon at least 2 weeks ahead. Prices double on the day.</li>
<li><strong>Restaurant timing:</strong> kitchens close 14:00–19:00. Plan or you'll be eating crêpes.</li>
<li><strong>The Louvre</strong> is not skippable; the Mona Lisa room is.</li>
<li><strong>Provence rentals:</strong> car is better than train for the smaller hill villages.</li>
</ul>
<h2 id="section-3">What I'd write more about</h2>
<ul>
<li>Why Aix-en-Provence is the right Provence base.</li>
<li>A piece on the French refusal to be hurried.</li>
<li>Paris museum strategy for people who don't actually like museums.</li>
</ul>
<p>

    day with photos, specific places, and longer reflections as I revisit my notes.
  </p>
