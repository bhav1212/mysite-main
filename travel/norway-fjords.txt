<p class="post-typo-sub">Six Days of Fjords, Trains, and Bread</p>

<p>Norway is the country where I most often forgot to take photos because I kept getting absorbed into the view. Fjords don't really work in a frame; they're about scale, and a phone screen doesn't have scale. The route below is the standard 'Norway in a Nutshell' loop with a couple of small additions.</p>
<h2 id="section-1">Route, day by day</h2>
<p><strong>Day 1 — Oslo.</strong> Land. Vigeland Park, the Opera House roof, dinner at a stalwart Norwegian place. Don't try to fit a museum in on day one.</p>
<p><strong>Day 2 — Oslo → Bergen.</strong> The Bergen Railway: 7 hours through the Hardangervidda plateau. Window seat on the south side. This is the journey people remember more than the cities.</p>
<p><strong>Days 3–4 — Bergen.</strong> Bryggen's old wooden warehouses (touristy but pretty). The Fløibanen funicular for the city overview. A day-trip on the fjord — Mostraumen is the closer option, the Naerøyfjord requires more travel.</p>
<p><strong>Day 5 — Flåm and the fjords.</strong> Bergen → Voss → Flåm. The Flåm Railway is short, steep, and dramatic. A boat through the Aurlandsfjord pays back the effort.</p>
<p><strong>Day 6 — Return to Oslo.</strong> Stalheim, the Stegastein viewpoint, then back to Oslo by train. Long day, worth it.</p>
<h2 id="section-2">Practical notes</h2>
<ul>
<li><strong>Norway in a Nutshell</strong> is the official packaged version of this loop. You can DIY for less, but the packaged tickets are convenient.</li>
<li><strong>Cost:</strong> Norway is the most expensive country I've travelled in. Plan groceries; a sandwich for dinner is honourable.</li>
<li><strong>Weather:</strong> bring layers regardless of month. The fjords have their own micro-climate.</li>
<li><strong>Bread:</strong> seriously. The dark, dense breads at any supermarket are genuinely better than 90% of what's labelled artisanal elsewhere.</li>
<li><strong>Light:</strong> in summer you'll have 18 hours of daylight; in winter, 6. Plan trip month around what you want.</li>
</ul>
<h2 id="section-3">What I'd write more about</h2>
<ul>
<li>Why the Bergen Railway is the trip you came for.</li>
<li>A comparison of fjord boat options for someone short on time.</li>
<li>Oslo's quiet case: an underrated capital.</li>
</ul>

    day with photos, specific places, and longer reflections as I revisit my notes.
