<p class="post-typo-sub">Four Days in Dubai (and a Day in Abu Dhabi)</p>

<p>The UAE is the only place I've been that feels engineered for the angle a camera will hold. Every viewpoint is a viewpoint. The route below tries to balance the iconic stuff with the parts of the country that surprised me — mostly the desert.</p>
<h2 id="section-1">Route, day by day</h2>
<p><strong>Day 1 — Old Dubai.</strong> Land, settle in. Afternoon in Deira and Bur Dubai: the souks (gold and spice), the Dubai Museum, an abra crossing on Dubai Creek. Dinner in Al Fahidi.</p>
<p><strong>Day 2 — New Dubai.</strong> Burj Khalifa at sunset (book the 124th-floor slot, not the 148th — the views are 90% the same and the queue half as long). Dubai Mall fountain show after. Evening walk along Marina.</p>
<p><strong>Day 3 — Desert.</strong> Dune safari with a reputable operator: 4×4 morning, lunch + camel ride, sunset over the dunes. Worth booking with a smaller group operator (15+ people kills the mood). Back in Dubai for dinner.</p>
<p><strong>Day 4 — Abu Dhabi day trip.</strong> 1.5h drive each way. Sheikh Zayed Grand Mosque (free, but dress code is strict). Louvre Abu Dhabi for an afternoon. Optional: Yas Island.</p>
<h2 id="section-2">Practical notes</h2>
<ul>
<li><strong>Metro:</strong> Red Line covers most of the touristy stretch. Cheap, fast, fully air-conditioned. Not Uber unless you must.</li>
<li><strong>Friday is the new Sunday</strong> in the UAE — many businesses close early on Friday for prayer. Plan accordingly.</li>
<li><strong>Dress code:</strong> at malls and mosques, cover shoulders and knees. Cabs and beaches don't care.</li>
<li><strong>Alcohol:</strong> at licensed hotels and some restaurants; not in public, ever.</li>
<li><strong>Best months:</strong> November–March. Anything else is a sauna with a city view.</li>
</ul>
<h2 id="section-3">What I'd write more about</h2>
<ul>
<li>Why the desert is the most underrated part of a Dubai trip.</li>
<li>Dubai vs Abu Dhabi: a fair comparison from a one-trip visitor.</li>
<li>What the Burj Khalifa observation actually looks like vs. the marketing.</li>
</ul>
<p>
<strong>Note:</strong> Working draft based on memory and a route I'd recommend. I'll expand each
    day with photos, specific places, and longer reflections as I revisit my notes.
  </p>
