Privacy Policy

This privacy policy explains how personal data is processed when you visit my personal portfolio at bhaveshjain.com.

Last updated: 22 November 2026

Data controller and contact

The person responsible for this personal portfolio and its processing of personal data is Bhavesh Jain, Heilbronn, Germany. For privacy questions or to exercise your rights, contact me at hello@bhaveshjain.com.

This policy covers bhaveshjain.com. Linked services and other websites have their own privacy policies.

Hosting and security

This website is hosted on GitHub Pages. Cloudflare delivers its content and protects it against attacks and abusive traffic. When you visit, these services receive the technical connection data needed to serve the website, including your IP address, the time and target of your request, browser and device information, and the referring page if your browser sends it.

This data is processed to deliver the website, troubleshoot errors, and keep it secure. The legal basis is Article 6(1)(f) of the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR). My legitimate interest is the reliable and secure operation of this portfolio. Website traffic is encrypted using HTTPS.

Recipients of technical access data include GitHub, Cloudflare, and their service providers. Further details about processing and retention are available in the GitHub Privacy Statement and the Cloudflare Privacy Policy.

Processing outside the EU

GitHub and Cloudflare may also process data in the United States and other countries outside the EU or European Economic Area. Both providers describe their participation in the EU-U.S. Data Privacy Framework and their use of Standard Contractual Clauses for international data transfers in their privacy policies.

Where a transfer falls under the EU-U.S. Data Privacy Framework, the relevant adequacy decision provides the legal basis under Article 45 GDPR. Other transfers may rely on the safeguards described by the providers, particularly Standard Contractual Clauses under Article 46 GDPR. Details about these safeguards are available through the provider policies linked above.

Cookies and locally hosted content

The portfolio application uses no analytics or advertising trackers and stores no preferences in cookies, Local Storage, or Session Storage. Depending on its security checks, Cloudflare may set technically necessary cookies, for example to recognise a successfully completed bot check.

Access to your device for strictly necessary security functions is based on Section 25(2)(2) of the German Telecommunications and Digital Services Data Protection Act (TDDDG). Related processing of personal data to secure the website is based on Article 6(1)(f) GDPR. Details about the purpose and lifetime of these cookies are available in Cloudflare cookie documentation. You can delete or block cookies in your browser; doing so may require you to complete security checks again.

Fonts, images, the introduction video, and downloadable files are delivered through this website itself. No external video players, maps, or social media plugins are embedded.

Email contact

This website has no contact form. Email links open your own email application. If you write to me, I process your email address, the information you provide, and any attachments to handle and answer your enquiry. The services used to send and receive email are involved in this processing.

The legal basis is Article 6(1)(f) GDPR, particularly my legitimate interest in responding to your enquiry. If an enquiry concerns entering into or performing a contract, Article 6(1)(b) GDPR applies. Contacting me and providing additional information are voluntary.

Data retention

Personal data is retained only for as long as the relevant processing purpose requires. Retention of technical logs by the hosting and security providers depends on their operational and security purposes and the criteria described in their privacy policies.

Email enquiries are kept while they are being handled and, where relevant, during subsequent collaboration. Legal retention obligations or the establishment, exercise, or defence of legal claims may require longer retention. When these reasons no longer apply, the data is deleted.

External links

Links to services such as LinkedIn, GitHub, publications, or my institutional profile lead to independently operated websites. When you open one of these links, its provider processes the connection data it receives under its own privacy policy.

Your rights

Subject to the applicable legal conditions, your rights include:

  • Access to information about the processing of your personal data (Article 15 GDPR),
  • Correction of inaccurate data (Article 16 GDPR),
  • Erasure and restriction of processing (Articles 17 and 18 GDPR),
  • Data portability where its conditions are met (Article 20 GDPR).

Right to object: Where processing is based on Article 6(1)(f) GDPR, you may object on grounds relating to your particular situation under Article 21 GDPR. Send your objection or any other privacy request to hello@bhaveshjain.com.

You also have the right to complain to a data protection supervisory authority under Article 77 GDPR, particularly the authority where you live or work, or where you believe an infringement has occurred. In Baden-Württemberg, this is the State Commissioner for Data Protection and Freedom of Information Baden-Württemberg.
